About Me

I'm a Staff Engineer at Apple, working on enterprise solutions for businesses and schools. I got to present at WWDC 2023 about new products we're building for enterprise customers. The talk is called "Do more with Managed Apple IDs" and you can watch it here.

Before Apple, I was on the Search Relevancy team at Salesforce, working on personalization for Einstein Search. I built features and services for A/B testing to improve search ranking. Before that, I helped create and deploy a distributed system to manage thousands of search servers.

I did my MS in Information Networking at Carnegie Mellon, focusing on Distributed Systems. I'm fascinated by how large-scale distributed systems are built and how they achieve scalability, caching, and fault-tolerance. I also have a certificate in Big Data from University of Washington.

In a previous life, I was a hardware tinkerer.
