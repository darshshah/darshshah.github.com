About Me

I'm a Staff Engineer at Apple, working on Apple Business, building enterprise solutions for businesses and schools. I am a World Wide Developer Conference (WWDC) 2023 speaker. My talk, "Do more with Managed Apple IDs," covers the new products we built for enterprise customers. You can watch it here.

Before Apple, I was on the Search Relevancy team at Salesforce, working on personalization for Einstein Search. I built features and services for A/B testing to improve search ranking. Before that, I helped create and deploy a distributed system to manage thousands of search servers.

I have an MS in Information Networking from Carnegie Mellon, where I focused on Distributed Systems. I'm fascinated by how large-scale systems achieve scalability, caching, and fault-tolerance. I also have a certificate in Big Data Technologies from the University of Washington.

Outside of work, I've run a marathon, visited 25+ countries, enjoy adventurous activities, and mentored at Hackbright Academy.

In a previous life, I was a hardware tinkerer.
